How to play

Drag back, let go

Pull away from the car like a catapult and release. The further you pull the harder it goes: blue is gentle, green safe, yellow brisk, red risky.

Three flicks a turn

Each turn is three flicks, the leader first. The nose only turns so far per flick, and the sharper the turn the gentler the flick, so end each one pointing where the next should go.

Flip · back to where the flick started
Tip · halfway along the flick
Off track · back to the last point on it

Keep the rubber side down

A car on its roof, on its side or off the track is put back and the flick is spent. Being knocked over by a rival costs nothing: you are righted where you lie.
